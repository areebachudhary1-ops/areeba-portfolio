About Me

I am a BS Software Engineering graduate from the University of Mianwali, interested in web development, mobile applications, UI/UX design, and AI-powered technology.

During my academic journey, I developed an interest in both technology and visual creativity. This led me to explore application development, UI/UX design, graphic design, branding, and AI-powered solutions.

My academic and personal projects include a LinkedIn clone, a Food Ordering App Software Requirements Specification, an MCB Bank website evaluation, and a C++ Age Calculator. My Final Year Project is NeuroCast, an AI-based epileptic seizure forecasting research system exploring graph transformers and brain connectivity with EEG data.

During my internship at OkraSoft, I designed screens for several mobile applications in Figma. This experience strengthened my interest in UI/UX design and mobile application development.

I also participated in a Devsinc Islamabad industrial visit to learn about software industry practices and professional development.
